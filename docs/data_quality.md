# Data Quality & Limitations Log

One entry per issue. Reference the `dataset_id` from [data_catalog.md](data_catalog.md).
These entries feed the "Limitations" sections of `reports/pN_*.md`.

| dataset_id | Issue | Impact on analysis | Handling | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ine_median_price_sqm | Two methodologies: 2018 (1Q2016–3Q2021, NUTS 2013) and 2022 (4Q2019–1Q2026, NUTS 2024); the series published under NUTS 2013 (0011364) stops at 4Q2023 | Tested on the 4Q2019–3Q2021 overlap (2,287 municipality-quarters): median ratio 2022-method / 2018-method = 1.002 in every quarter, individual municipalities within about ±5% (p10 0.945, p90 1.052). No level shift, only municipal noise | Use the 2022 series from 4Q2019 and the 2018 series for 1Q2016–3Q2019; keep a `method` column and report the municipal noise as a limitation | 2026-10-21 |

| ine_median_rent_sqm | Median rent is published only where a municipality-year has at least 33 new leases (smallest published count = 33); 30–38% of municipality-years are blank (2020: 38%, 2024: 31%), mostly small inland municipalities, and the lease count is suppressed too | Rent-based indicators (RTI) cover a selected, more urban subsample; rent models are biased toward larger markets | Never impute rent from the blanks silently; report coverage next to RTI results; consider NUTS III rents or a modelled rent for sensitivity checks | 2026-10-21 |

| ine_median_price_sqm | In the 2022-method series 21% of municipality-quarters are blank overall (Total 4%, Existing 6%, New 52%) | Prices for new dwellings exist for under half of municipality-quarters | Use the Total and Existing categories as the main price; treat New as supplementary | 2026-10-21 |

| panel_municipality_year | Indicators exist only where a price (303 of 308 municipalities in 2024) and an income are published; rent-based ones where at least 33 new leases (about 69% of municipalities in 2024) | Subsamples differ by indicator and shift toward larger markets over time | Always report the number of municipalities behind each statistic | 2026-10-21 |
| panel_municipality_year | `al_survivor_units_n` is the cumulative number of today's registered units by registration year (survivors), a stock; the 2026 snapshot (`al_units_snapshot_n`) adds 7,844 units registered in 2026 | The series understates past stock and is not a flow | Use for cross-section (snapshot) first; time variation only as a lower bound | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_construction | The bedroom split of completed dwellings is incomplete before 2003: the sum of sizes is below the total in 2,121 municipality-years of 1995-2002 (9.7% of dwellings), never above; since 2003 only 10 cells (60 of 767,980 dwellings, 0.008%) differ, mostly by 1; licensed dwellings reconcile exactly | Completions by size are biased before 2003 | Use `size == 'total'` for completions before 2003; size splits only from 2003 | 2026-10-21 |
