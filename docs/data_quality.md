# Data Quality & Limitations Log

One entry per issue. Reference the `dataset_id` from [data_catalog.md](data_catalog.md).
These entries feed the "Limitations" sections of `reports/pN_*.md`.

| dataset_id | Issue | Impact on analysis | Handling | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ine_median_price_sqm | Two methodologies: 2018 (1Q2016–3Q2021, NUTS 2013) and 2022 (4Q2019–1Q2026, NUTS 2024); the series published under NUTS 2013 (0011364) stops at 4Q2023 | A level shift at the splice is possible; the overlap is only 4Q2019–3Q2021 | Use 2022 series as primary, compare both on the overlap and report the gap before using the 2016–2019 years | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_median_rent_sqm | INE rent series (Methodology 2021) start in 2020, not 2017 | Municipal rent-to-income (RTI) limited to 2020–2024 | Use annual 0012600; use 0012601 counts to flag thin cells; look for pre-2020 series | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_median_rent_sqm | Median rent is published only where a municipality-year has at least 33 new leases (smallest published count = 33); 30–38% of municipality-years are blank (2020: 38%, 2024: 31%), mostly small inland municipalities, and the lease count is suppressed too | Rent-based indicators (RTI) cover a selected, more urban subsample; rent models are biased toward larger markets | Never impute rent from the blanks silently; report coverage next to RTI results; consider NUTS III rents or a modelled rent for sensitivity checks | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_* (all API series) | Responses mix municipalities, parishes and NUTS III rows; suppressed cells return `-`; Census series use plain 4-char municipality codes instead of NUTS III prefix + code | Wrong row counts or false zeros if not filtered | Take `geocod[3:]` for 7-char codes (4-char as is), validate against the CAOP municipality list, treat `-` as missing | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_* (all API series) | Some indicators add city rows (e.g. 'Coimbra', 'Amadora' by city) with 7-char codes that look like municipalities | Double counting or a wrong muni_code if taken at face value | Filter with `harmonize.keep_municipalities`; the three series checked (0012600, 0012234, 0012248) had no unmatched municipality-level geographies | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_* (all API series) | Same indicator is republished per NUTS vintage (2013 vs 2024); some municipal codes/names differ | Panel breaks at splices, mismatched keys | Map through `data/external/muni_crosswalk.csv` and prefer the NUTS 2024 vintage | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_hpi | Indicator 0014765 is national only (split by existing / new dwellings), no regional detail | No regional HPI from this source | Use municipal sale-price medians for regional work; HPI for national time series and Eurostat comparison | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_income | Two vintages: 2015–2021 (NUTS 2013) and 2018–2024 (NUTS 2024); unit is the tax household, not the person | Ratios to household prices/rents mix units; overlap years may differ | Use 2024-vintage series, check the 2018–2021 overlap, state the unit in indicator definitions | 2026-10-21 |
| ine_bank_valuation | Small municipalities are suppressed in a given month (publishing threshold around 33 valuations) and values are appraisals, not transactions | Many missing municipality-months; level may differ from transaction prices | Aggregate to year or quarter; compare with the transaction median | 2026-10-21 |
| aima_foreigners | INE series ends in 2023 because AIMA delayed its data; later municipal figures exist only in AIMA PDF reports | No municipal foreign-population series after 2023 | Use 2014–2023; census 2021 and population estimates as a cross-check | 2026-10-21 |
| rnal_al | Current registry is a snapshot of live registrations; cancelled units are absent, and the older dados.gov.pt copies are archived | Counting units by `DataRegisto` understates the past stock (survivorship) | Treat as a lower bound for earlier years; if possible, store dated snapshots from now on | 2026-10-21 |
| at_imi_imt | No official machine-readable municipal IMI/IMT series found; only third-party IMI rates for 2023–2025 | Fiscal simulator lacks verified municipal IMI/IMT inputs | Search DGAL / AT statistics; fall back to national totals plus documented assumptions | 2026-10-21 |
